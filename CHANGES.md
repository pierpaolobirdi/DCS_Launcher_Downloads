- **The small buttons look lit only when pressing them just works.** Once something was launched, **No Tacview** is
  dimmed (like LAUNCH, which says "LAUNCHED") and **DCS Only** is dimmed while DCS runs. When the DCS the launcher
  started has ended and the apps are still open, DCS Only is lit again and No Tacview stays dimmed but clickable (it asks
  first, like LAUNCH). When everything is closed they all go back to normal. Without Tacview (or with its recording
  off) No Tacview stays greyed out and disabled.
